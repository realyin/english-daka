# 短文课（Passage）设计

> 新课型：一篇 4–6 句的小短文，介绍自己 / 喜欢的动物 / 喜欢的东西 / 一天的作息。
> 没有问答，孩子**看图自己说**。样例四篇：My Morning Routine · I like rabbits ·
> About me · This is an apple。文本以线下课为准，这份只定「怎么放进 app」。
>
> 家长定的形态：**一篇短文一张图**（图内分格），图和文字各在一边，
> 文字像图的说明。不要一句一图，短文本来就短，拆散了有割裂感。

---

## 一、定位：它和现有五种课型差在哪

| | 现有课型 | 短文课 |
|---|---|---|
| 单位 | 一卡 = 一个词 | **一卡 = 一篇短文** |
| 图 | 一卡一张单主体图 | 一篇一张**拼格大图**（2–5 格） |
| 学一学 | 提问气泡 + 回答气泡 | 图一边、句子一边，句句可点 |
| 闯关 / 考一考 | 三选一 / 哪一个是 X | **不进**（没有题干，也没有唯一答案） |
| 孩子做什么 | 听问、答 | 先听整篇，再遮字看图自己说 |

结论：不要把短文塞进 `dialog` 的 q/a 里。卡片加一个和 `dialog` 平行的 `lines`
字段，app 见到 `lines` 就按短文渲染。

## 二、目录位置

- **一篇短文 = 一节课 = 一张卡**（家长定：目录上一篇一张封面，不合并到一起）。
  `level: K2`，`group: "短文 Passages"`，`seq` 落 8xx（学习路径末尾：会认词 → 会拼 → 会连句说；
  原来留给绘本的 8xx 改给短文，绘本顺延 9xx）。
- 课程 id `k2-passage-<篇名>`，封面就是这一篇的拼格图。四篇：

| id | seq | title |
|---|---|---|
| `k2-passage-morning-routine` | 801 | My Morning Routine · 我的早晨 |
| `k2-passage-rabbits` | 802 | I Like Rabbits · 我喜欢兔子 |
| `k2-passage-about-me` | 803 | About Me · 自我介绍 |
| `k2-passage-apple` | 804 | This Is an Apple · 苹果 |

- 新短文用 `python new_lesson.py passage --title "篇名 · 中文" --badge 🐰` 建课，
  `gen_audio.py --date` 盖上课日期。

## 三、数据结构

```jsonc
{
  "seq": 801, "level": "K2", "group": "短文 Passages", "badge": "🌅",
  "title": "My Morning Routine · 我的早晨",
  "cover": "images/ps-morning-routine.webp",
  "cards": [                             // 只有这一张
    {
      "word": "My Morning Routine",      // 短文标题，卡头显示；一课内不重复
      "cn": "我的早晨",
      "tag": "passage",                  // 新 tag：app / check_lesson 靠它识别
      "image": "images/ps-morning-routine.webp",
      "collage": true,                   // 拼格图，照旧标上
      "lines": [                         // 代替 dialog。一条 = 一句，顺序 = 图的阅读顺序
        { "t": "*First*, I get up.",              "cn": "首先，我起床。" },
        { "t": "I get up every morning.",         "cn": "我每天早上都起床。" },
        { "t": "*Then*, I wash my face.",         "cn": "然后，我洗脸。" },
        { "t": "I wash my face with water. My face is clean.", "cn": "我用水洗脸。我的脸干干净净。" },
        { "t": "*Next*, I brush my teeth.",       "cn": "接着，我刷牙。" },
        { "t": "I brush my teeth with a toothbrush. My teeth are white.", "cn": "我用牙刷刷牙。我的牙齿白白的。" },
        { "t": "*Last*, I take a shower.",        "cn": "最后，我洗澡。" },
        { "t": "I take a shower every day. I am fresh.", "cn": "我每天洗澡。我清清爽爽。" },
        { "t": "After my morning routine, I am *ready for school*!", "cn": "做完早晨的事，我准备好上学啦！" }
      ]
    },
  ]
}
```

字段约定：

| 字段 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| `lines[].t` | 句子。`*…*` 包住的是**重点词**，渲染成彩色（对应课件里的彩字），TTS 合成前去掉星号。一行可以放两个短句（同一格的说明写一起） |
| `lines[].cn` | 中文，同现有 `a_cn` |
| `lines[].audio` | **脚本回写**，`gen_audio.py` 用 `a` 角色（Jenny）合成，命名规则同答句 |
| `word` | 短文标题，不是词，但一课内唯一即可（考一考本来就不进） |
| `quiz` / `practice` | 不用写。`tag: "passage"` 的卡默认既不进考一考也不进闯关 |

`t` 里可以照常写 `/r/` 音标记号（"Rabbit begins with the sound /r/."），走
`gen_audio.py` 现成的拼接路径。这也是短文用 **Jenny** 不用童声 Ana 的原因：音素库是
Jenny，混童声一句话两个音色；而且现有体系里「孩子要说的句子 = 回答 = Jenny」，
短文整篇都是孩子要说的话，口径一致。

## 四、学一学怎么呈现

卡片头不变（缩略图 + 标题 + 中文）。展开后是**图一边、字一边**：

```
┌──────────────┬───────────────────────┐
│              │ ① First, I get up.    │   宽屏（iPad / 横屏 / 720 列）：
│   拼格大图   │   I get up every …    │   图占左 45%，句子在右，
│   (3:4)      │ ② Then, I wash …      │   句子列表是图的说明
│              │ ③ Next, …             │
│              │ ④ Last, …             │
│              │ ★ After my morning …  │
└──────────────┴───────────────────────┘

┌──────────────────────┐
│       拼格大图        │   手机竖屏：图在上占满宽，
├──────────────────────┤   句子在下。仍然是「图一块、字一块」，
│ ① First, I get up.   │   不拆成一句一图
│   …                  │
└──────────────────────┘
```

1. **图放大**。现有 `.pic` 最大 200px 是给单词图用的，短文的拼格图要能看清每一格：
   宽屏约 300px 宽，手机满宽。点图可全屏放大（现有闯关/考一考有没有大图查看，实现时看，
   没有就加一个简单的 lightbox）。
2. **句子编号**。每行前面一个彩色圆号 ①②③④，颜色按顺序四色轮换，和图的阅读顺序
   （从左到右、从上到下）对应。同一格的两句共用一个号。
3. 句子行就是现有 `.sline`：点句播句、长按查词、点空白连播，全部复用。
   气泡角色标签叫「🗣 我来说」，不叫「回答」。
4. **重点词彩色**：`*First*` 渲染成带色 `<b>`。
5. 卡片内两个按钮，只在短文卡出现：
   - **▶ 整篇连读**：按行顺序播完，播到哪行哪行高亮。这是「先听整篇」。
   - **🙈 遮字**：隐藏全部句子文字，只留编号和图；孩子看图说，点编号揭开一句核对。
     这是「看图自己说」，也是这一课的主练法。
6. **不做录音**（v1）。MediaRecorder 在 iOS PWA 里权限和回放都有坑，先用「遮字」
   达成看图说；家长要的话再加。

## 五、闯关 / 考一考 / 打卡 / 星星

- `askable()` 按 `dialog` 过滤，短文卡没有 `dialog`，闯关自然空；`quizCards()` 同理。
  进入这两个模式时判断「本课没有可练的题」，把入口按钮隐藏（0 题课会不会白屏，实现时要测）。
- **打卡**（家长已定）：**听满一篇短文 = 打卡**。每句都点过、或「整篇连读」放完，
  就算听满；一篇按 3 张卡入账（展开记 1 张，听满补足到 3），日历深浅照这个折算，
  同一篇一天只算一次。实现在 `app.html` 的 `notePassageDone`。
- **星星**：现在只有答题得星，短文课听完整篇、遮字说完都不得星。家长定的口径，先不动；
  如果觉得该给（比如「遮字说完一篇 +1 星」），再单独定。

## 六、需要改的脚本

| 文件 | 改动 |
|---|---|
| `new_lesson.py` | 加 `passage` 课型：`--title` 给短文标题，句子留「待填」；seq 段 800；生成一张拼格图的 prompt |
| `gen_audio.py` | 遍历 `cards[].lines[].t`（去星号）按 `a` 角色合成，回写 `lines[].audio` |
| `check_lesson.py` | `tag: passage` 的卡允许无 `dialog`、必须有 `lines`；`*` 成对；`lines[].audio` 齐 |

| `app.html` | `buildDialog` 分支（左右/上下布局、编号、大图）、整篇连读、遮字、隐藏闯关/考一考入口；bump `sw.js` 版本 |
| `index.html` | 组色表加「短文 Passages」 |

## 七、配图：一篇一张拼格图

**规则**：一篇一张 **3:4 竖图**（和入库规范一致，宽屏放左边刚好），图内分 2–5 格，
**格子的阅读顺序 = 句子顺序**（从左到右、从上到下），**图里不要任何文字**（句子由 app
渲染）。四篇的主角必须是同一个小女孩，所以每条 prompt 都带人设。

风格前缀（沿用 `new_lesson.py` 的 `STYLE_PREFIX`，只把「NO panel dividers」换成
允许分格的写法）：

```
Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolor style with clean rounded
outlines, warm bright cheerful colors, simple uncluttered backgrounds, friendly
and cute, suitable for a 5-year-old, vertical 3:4 composition. The image is
divided into clearly separated panels with thin soft white gutters and gently
rounded corners, each panel a single clear scene. NO text, NO letters, NO words,
NO numbers, NO speech bubbles, NO labels, NO outer border.
```

主角人设（每条都带）：

```
The main character is Mumu: a 5-year-old girl with shoulder-length dark brown
hair, a pink headband with a small pink bow on the left, big round dark eyes,
rosy cheeks, wearing a pink short-sleeved top with a white collar. Exactly the
same character in every panel and every image.
```

负面提示：

```
text, letters, words, numbers, captions, labels, watermark, speech bubble,
thick black comic borders, realistic photo, scary, cluttered, extra characters,
different girl, inconsistent hair or clothes
```

下面每条 = `前缀 + 人设 + 画面描述`。「存为」名 = 卡片 `image` 名 = 入库文件名。
入库照旧 `python ingest_images.py <目录>`（3:4 直接出，居中裁剪不会切到格子）。

### `ps-morning-routine` — My Morning Routine

> Layout: five panels — a 2×2 grid on top and one wide panel across the bottom.
> Reading order: top-left, top-right, middle-left, middle-right, bottom.
> Panel 1: Mumu sitting up in bed just after waking, stretching both arms high
> with a happy yawn, pink polka-dot pajamas, morning sunlight through a window.
> Panel 2: Mumu at a bathroom sink splashing clear water onto her face with both
> hands, eyes closed and smiling, a pink towel hanging beside her.
> Panel 3: Mumu brushing her teeth with a blue toothbrush, white foam at the
> corner of her mouth, holding a small purple cup.
> Panel 4: Mumu in a shower with warm water falling from a silver shower head,
> hair wet, holding a yellow sponge, soft bubbles, a small yellow rubber duck;
> shown from the shoulders up, child-appropriate.
> Panel 5 (wide): Mumu outdoors in a pink school shirt with a purple backpack,
> waving at the viewer with a big smile, a small orange schoolhouse and green
> trees behind her, bright blue sky.

### `ps-rabbits` — I Like Rabbits

> Layout: one large panel filling the top two-thirds, and a row of three small
> square panels across the bottom. Reading order: large panel first, then the
> three small ones left to right.
> Large panel: Mumu in a blue denim overall dress over her pink top, kneeling on
> green grass in a sunny garden, one hand waving hello at the viewer, the other
> arm gently hugging a fluffy white rabbit, a few small pink hearts floating
> around them, white picket fence and small pink flowers behind.
> Small panel 1: a single fluffy white rabbit sitting sideways on the grass so
> its two tall upright ears and its small round cotton tail are both clearly
> visible, soft fur texture, a carrot beside it, no people.
> Small panel 2: a big brown cartoon dog barking loudly behind a low wooden
> fence, mouth open, Mumu a few steps away holding her hands up near her chest
> and leaning back with a worried face; cartoon and mild, not frightening.
> Small panel 3: Mumu facing the viewer with both palms open at her sides in a
> friendly asking pose, head tilted and smiling, a small cat, a small bird and a
> small fish sitting in a row on the grass in front of her.

（"Rabbit begins with the sound /r/." 这句不单独配格，说的时候指着大图的兔子。）

### `ps-about-me` — About Me

> Layout: one large panel filling the top half, and a 2×2 grid of four small
> panels in the bottom half. Reading order: large panel first, then the small
> ones top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right.
> Large panel: Mumu standing straight and waving hello with one hand raised,
> wearing a pink pinafore dress over a white t-shirt, white socks and pink
> sneakers, plain pale pink background with a few floating hearts and stars.
> Small panel 1: Mumu holding up her whole open hand showing five fingers with a
> proud smile, a small round birthday cake with exactly five lit candles beside
> her.
> Small panel 2: Mumu standing between her mom and dad holding hands with both,
> mom with shoulder-length dark hair in a yellow top, dad with short dark hair
> in a blue shirt, all three smiling.
> Small panel 3: Mumu holding a gray microphone with both hands, eyes closed,
> mouth open in song, one foot lifted as if dancing, colorful music notes
> floating around her.
> Small panel 4: Mumu standing confidently with one hand on her chest and the
> other pointing up, mouth open mid-speech and beaming, a small globe on a desk
> beside her.

（"Thank you!" 不单独配格。）

### `ps-apple` — This Is an Apple

> Layout: one large panel filling the top two-thirds, and a row of two small
> panels across the bottom. Reading order: large panel first, then bottom-left,
> bottom-right.
> Large panel: Mumu holding up one shiny red apple with a green leaf in one hand
> and pointing at it with the other, smiling at the viewer, a woven basket full
> of red apples on a wooden table in front of her, apple orchard with green
> trees behind.
> Small panel 1: a single big shiny red apple with a short stem and one green
> leaf, perfectly round, sitting alone on a wooden table, soft highlight on its
> skin, no people.
> Small panel 2: Mumu taking a happy bite out of a small red apple, eyes
> squeezed shut with delight, rosy cheeks, a small bite mark visible on the
> apple.

出图后检查两件事：四张图里的女孩是不是同一个人（发型、发箍、衣服）；每格是不是
真的空白无字。AI 拼格图很爱在格子里塞标题，有字就重出。

## 八、还要家长定的

1. **短文课给不给星星**（见第五节）。默认先不给。
2. **句子文本是否完全照课件**。按「教什么跟线下课走」，默认原样；
   唯一改动是 "I am _5_ years old." 写成了 "I am five years old."（课件是填空，app 里要念出来）。
3. 「我来说」这个角色标签的叫法。
